# 02 — Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

## Level 1 Phases
1. Pre-construction
2. Design and engineering
3. Permits and approvals
4. Procurement and contracting
5. Site prep and foundation
6. Superstructure (frame/roof)
7. MEP systems
8. Interior finishes
9. Exterior works
10. Testing, inspection, handover
